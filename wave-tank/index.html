    Numerical wave tank¶

    A verified 2D flume and 3D tank implemented in DualSPHysics, a GPU-accelerated weakly compressible SPH (WCSPH) solver. The tank is supplied ready for testing; the user's structure, device and test matrix are implemented during set-up.

    Facility sheet¶

    Values describe the default configuration. Domain dimensions, water depth, bathymetry and instrumentation are adapted to each campaign during scoping.

    Item Specification
    Solver DualSPHysics, WCSPH on GPU (CUDA)
    Configurations 2-D flume; 3-D tank or basin
    Wavemakers Piston and flap; first-order and second-order (Madsen) generation
    Wave conditions Regular, irregular (JONSWAP, Pierson–Moskowitz), focused, solitary, multisine, user-defined paddle signals
    Absorption Active wave absorption at the paddle (AWAS); passive damping zones; optional open boundaries
    Rigid bodies 6-DoF floating bodies; constraints, joints and linear springs, or dampers via Project Chrono
    Moorings Lumped-mass mooring lines via MoorDyn+
    Instruments Free-surface gauges, pressure and velocity probes, integrated forces and moments, body kinematics
    Outputs CSV time series; VTK particle and iso-surface fields for ParaView

    Wave generation and absorption¶

    Paddle motion is derived from the Biésel transfer function for the target wave field, with second-order correction to suppress spurious free long waves where required. Irregular sea states are synthesised from the target spectrum; focused waves use NewWave-type groups with prescribed focus time and location. Reflection is controlled by active absorption at the paddle and passive damping at the far end, and checked with three-gauge reflection analysis. In addition, customized free-surface elevation can be used to command the motion of the piston or flap generator.

    Resolution guidance¶

    Criterion Typical starting point
    Particles per wave height, \(H/dp\) ≥ 8 for propagation; finer for breaking and impact
    Particles across the smallest body dimension Set by the load or motion of interest, and confirmed by convergence
    Damping zone length ≥ 1–2 wavelengths
    Smoothing length \(h = 1.3\times \text{coefh}\,\sqrt{d}\,dp\), with \(d\) the number of dimensions

    Final values are confirmed by convergence studies on the quantities that matter for the campaign.

    Required material¶

    • Geometry (STL/OBJ, or a drawing with dimensions), mass properties for floating bodies, and mooring layout if relevant.
    • The test matrix, specifying wave conditions, water depth, scale, and duration of each run.
    • The quantities to be measured and their required accuracy.
    • Experimental or reference data for validation, if available.
    • A preliminary working model, for example built from our downloadable tanks.

    Typical campaigns¶

    Campaign Key outputs
    Wave energy converters 6-DoF motions, PTO response, absorbed power, capture width ratio, mooring loads
    Breaking waves on monopiles Slamming and inline forces, impact pressures, run-up
    Focused waves on moored structures Peak motions, mooring tensions and snap loads, air gap
    Multisine tests Transfer functions, coherence, RAOs
